1. Overview

YUKI DREAM (operated by Yuki Mutoh) is committed to protecting privacy. This policy explains how we handle information in connection with our music content and TikTok integration.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information from visitors to this website. Our TikTok Content Posting API integration operates on our own account only and does not collect, process, or store data from other TikTok users.

3. TikTok API Usage

We use TikTok's official Content Posting API to upload video content to our TikTok account (@yukihira.1967). The API access is limited to:

  • video.upload — uploading videos to our own account
  • user.info.basic — verifying our own account identity

No data from other TikTok users is accessed or stored. Access tokens are stored securely on our local systems and are not shared.

4. Third-Party Services

Our music is distributed via TuneCore Japan. Videos are generated using Kling AI. These services have their own privacy policies.

5. Cookies

This website does not use cookies or any tracking technologies.

6. Data Retention

We retain API access tokens only as long as necessary for posting content. Tokens are deleted when no longer needed.
